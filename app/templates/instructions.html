How to Play

Master the rules of Super Tic-Tac-Toe

  • 1
    The board has 9 sub-boards (3x3), forming one large 3x3 grid.
  • 2
    Play vs AI or Player vs Player on the same device. X always goes first.
  • 3
    When you click a cell, your opponent is sent to the corresponding sub-board.
  • 4
    If that sub-board is already won or full, your opponent can move anywhere.
  • 5
    Win a sub-board like regular tic-tac-toe — three in a row.
  • 6
    Win the meta board by winning 3 sub-boards in a row!
